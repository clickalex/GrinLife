The one rule that makes this work

Never more than one product in active build at a time. A product in market is allowed; a product in construction is not.

Follow-on products die in R&D at a 45% higher rate than other project types — not from difficulty, but from resources being continuously pulled toward whichever product has paying customers. The gates exist to stop that drift.
