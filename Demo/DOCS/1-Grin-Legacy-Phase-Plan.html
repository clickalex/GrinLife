Grin Legacy

Guided family storytelling that ends in a beautiful printed book. The revenue engine of the portfolio — and the only track that bills a customer with zero network.

Status: BUILD FIRST  ·  Funds Waves 2 and 3
The Product in One Page
What it isA weekly prompt arrives on WhatsApp. A parent or grandparent answers by voice. AI transcribes and lightly shapes it into prose. After ~30 stories, the family receives a hardcover book with QR codes that play the original voice.
Who buysAn adult child aged 35–55, buying for a parent aged 60–85. Buyer ≠ user. This is a gift purchase with an emotional trigger and a deadline.
Trigger eventsBirthdays (esp. 60/70/75/80), Diwali, retirement, a diagnosis, a grandchild's birth, a death in the extended family, NRI children visiting home.
Why it winsFull value delivered to user #1 with an empty network. Competitors charge $59–$1,500 today, proving willingness to pay. WhatsApp-voice + Indian-language delivery is a genuine, unserved wedge.
Core riskCompletion, not acquisition. If storytellers quit at story #6, you ship no books and refunds spike. Every phase below is designed around completion rate.
Phase 0 · Weeks 1–3 · Validation

Sell it before you build it

Zero code. The goal is a signed rupee, not a working product. If you cannot sell this manually to warm contacts, no amount of engineering fixes it.

BuildDetail

Fake-door testRun ₹15–25k of Meta/Instagram ads at NRI + tier-1 Indian audiences. Measure checkout-start rate, not clicks.
Concierge deliveryFirst 10 customers served entirely by hand. You personally WhatsApp the prompts, download the voice notes, run Whisper, edit the prose. Manual is the point — it's how you learn the prompts.

Phase 2 · Months 6–12 · Grow to Gate 1

Fix completion, then scale acquisition

In that order. Spending on ads while completion is at 40% is pouring money into a leaking bucket.

Completion levers (build first)

  • Nudge ladder — missed prompt triggers a gentle re-ask at 48h, then a different easier question at day 5. Never guilt.
  • Family cheerleading — when a story lands, notify relatives; their reactions go back to the storyteller. This is the single strongest completion lever.
  • Voice-call fallback — for storytellers who don't take to WhatsApp, a scheduled phone call where a human (later, an AI voice agent) asks the question.
  • Progress artifact — show the book filling up. Ten stories in, email a 10-page preview PDF. Loss aversion does the rest.

The last one is worth naming explicitly: it is an ethical minefield, it terrifies exactly the demographic you're selling to, and it would poison the trust the rest of the product depends on.

India compliance — what applies to Legacy

Legacy is the lightest of the three, but it is not zero. You are a Data Fiduciary under the DPDP Act 2023, processing highly personal family data.

DPDP Act 2023Full compliance required by 13 May 2027 under the phased rollout. Needed: clear consent notice (available in relevant Eighth Schedule languages), purpose limitation, breach notification, and honouring erasure requests. Penalties reach ₹250 crore for security failures.
Children's dataA "child" is anyone under 18 and requires verifiable parental consent. Family archives contain grandchildren — so gate any under-18 contributor behind a parent account. Simplest v1 answer: contributors must be 18+.
Deceased personsDPDP is largely silent here. Get contractual clarity in your ToS on who controls an archive after the storyteller dies — this is where family disputes will land.
Not an intermediaryLegacy is private, not user-to-user public content, so IT Rules 2021 intermediary duties largely don't bite. This changes completely in Wave 2.

Top risks

RiskMitigation
Storyteller abandons
Most likely failure		All four completion levers in Phase 2. Also: promise the book at 20 stories, not 52 — a shippable artifact beats a perfect one.
Print quality disappointsThe artifact is the product. Order samples from 3 printers before launch. Every book human-reviewed pre-print through at least the first 200.
AI mangles dialectWhisper is weak on Awadhi/Bhojpuri. Budget human transcription for Indic languages initially — and price that in rather than shipping garbage.
Storyteller dies mid-projectIt will happen. Have a compassionate protocol: pause billing immediately, offer to produce the book from whatever exists, free of charge. Handled well, these become your most powerful advocates.
WhatsApp policy changePlatform dependency is real. Keep an SMS + email + voice-call fallback path working from month 6.
