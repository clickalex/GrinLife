# GrinLuck Website — Design Direction

## Three Possible Directions

### 1. The Golden Orbit
**Very Brief Intro:** An editorial night-sky experience where human connection is represented by warm orbital paths and intimate pockets of light. It feels considered, humane, and quietly cinematic rather than technologically cold.

**Probability:** 0.07

### 2. The Social Field Guide
**Very Brief Intro:** A warm, print-inspired field manual that treats chance encounters as discoveries worth documenting. It uses tactile paper, annotations, and map-like marks to make the product feel approachable and intentional.

**Probability:** 0.04

### 3. The Roll Room
**Very Brief Intro:** A bold, high-contrast game-night direction built around physical dice, split screens, and kinetic decision moments. It feels more playful and immediate, with an energetic social pulse.

**Probability:** 0.09

## Chosen Direction — The Golden Orbit

### Design Movement

**Cinematic editorial futurism** with the emotional restraint of a premium magazine and the visual poetry of a star chart. The design turns GrinLuck's product logic into a human story: a small spark, a private conversation, and a shared orbit.

### Core Principles

1. **Warmth inside darkness:** The near-black canvas protects intimacy while warm amber marks signal safe possibility.
2. **Asymmetry with purpose:** Content moves along a vertical orbital thread instead of stacking in a conventional centered marketing grid.
3. **Disclosure through depth:** Dense product information is revealed through layered cards, side notes, and quiet micro-labels instead of loud sales copy.
4. **Human-scale technology:** Every interface motif should make the real-time product feel personal, consent-led, and calm.

### Color Philosophy

The background is **deep blue-black obsidian**, representing the unknown before a roll. **Solar amber** is GrinLuck’s owned signal color: it appears only where chance, consent, or connection occurs. Desaturated **moonstone**, **mist blue**, and a barely-there **orchid shadow** add depth without becoming a neon gradient. The palette communicates privacy first, then warmth.

### Layout Paradigm

The page is constructed as an **orbital journey**. A slim, luminous connection thread runs vertically through the experience. Sections alternate between an oversized left-aligned narrative block and offset right-side system cards, as if each product principle is a waypoint in an unfolding constellation. The hero is a wide cinematic canvas with content held low and left, leaving room for the unknown.

### Signature Elements

1. **Orbit lines:** Fine amber arcs and faint star-map dots connect sections and cards.
2. **The Chance Token:** A glowing, faceted dice-like mark appears at decision points and as the primary call-to-action icon.
3. **Conversation Capsules:** Translucent, asymmetric message-shaped panels hold product moments and safety commitments.

### Interaction Philosophy

Interaction should feel like making a considered choice, not chasing a reward. Buttons offer a subtle press response; cards lift gently when touched; menu links trace a thin amber underline. Any simulated product action explains what would happen rather than pretending that an unbuilt matchmaking service is active.

### Animation

The hero orbit rotates almost imperceptibly, while background stars drift at different speeds. Section content enters with a short opacity-and-translate reveal using a crisp ease-out curve; successive elements stagger by 50–70ms. Motion remains below 400ms, is limited to transform and opacity, and is disabled for reduced-motion preferences. There are no flashing counters, reward cascades, or compulsive looping mechanics.

### Typography System

**DM Serif Display** carries the human, reflective headlines; **Manrope** handles concise body copy and navigation; **IBM Plex Mono** marks system labels, timestamps, and product mechanics. Headlines use generous line-height and restrained italic emphasis. No Inter is used.

### Brand Essence

**GrinLuck is the private social space for people who want a real conversation before a profile.**

Personality: **serendipitous, protective, unforced**.

### Brand Voice

Headlines sound observant and inviting. CTAs are calm, clear, and consent-aware. Microcopy avoids hype, follower language, and artificial urgency.

Example lines:

> “A good conversation does not need an algorithm to begin.”

> “Roll when you are open to being surprised.”

### Wordmark & Logo

The mark is a **faceted amber orbit token**: a rounded geometric core with one broken circular path, suggesting a chance encounter becoming a shared path. The wordmark is set in a custom-feeling, widely tracked Manrope treatment with a small orbit dot replacing the inner counter of a letterform where appropriate.

### Signature Brand Color

**Solar Amber — #F2A43A**. It should always indicate a meaningful moment: a roll, an informed choice, or a mutual connection.
